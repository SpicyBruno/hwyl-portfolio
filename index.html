Manifesto
— § 01

Print
the unsayable.

Every poster is a small scripture: a fragment of an emotion too sharp to speak. Made by hand, printed in limited runs, signed in blood-red ink. HWYL is a one-person studio working at the intersection of editorial design, character illustration, and metal-adjacent art direction. No filler. Every piece earns its place on the page, and on your wall.
The Shop

Three editions.
Limited prints.

Heavy 250gsm matte paper. A2 format. Edition of 50, hand-numbered. Ships worldwide rolled in a kraft tube.

Process

Slow ink.
Sharp intent.

Every poster goes through the same four-stage ritual: concept, distortion, ink, paper. No shortcuts. No AI fill.

— 01

Brief & mood

We dig into the emotional core of the piece. Words, references, a colour temperature. No mood-board fluff; just the one image we're chasing.

— 02

Sketch & distortion

Line work, then deliberate corruption: torn type, bled ink, scorched grain. The ugly part is the part that matters.

— 03

Print & sign

250gsm matte stock, edition of 50, hand-numbered with a wax stamp on the back. Each one slightly different.

— 04

Ship

Rolled in a kraft tube with a hand-written tag. Tracked worldwide.
